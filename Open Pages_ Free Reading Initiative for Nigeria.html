Access should never be a barrier

There are stories waiting to be discovered. And minds waiting to grow.

Many people cannot afford to buy books. Educational resources are not equally accessible. Libraries and physical collections can be far away. Valuable Nigerian research is often hard to discover, and few people get the chance to build a steady reading habit.
